Executive Abstract

Financial time series analysis has long been trapped in a dichotomy between linear, oversimplified statistical models (e.g., ARIMA) and opaque, computationally expensive deep learning black boxes (e.g., LSTMs, Transformers). This paper introduces Kinetopus Engine, a parsimonious, White-Box Scientific Machine Learning (SciML) framework that challenges the assumption of pure stochastic randomness by constructing a mathematically justified continuous physical perspective over discrete financial data. Operating under low computational complexity on consumer-grade CPU hardware, the pipeline discovers explicit, inspectable ordinary differential equations $\mathrm{d}r/\mathrm{d}t = f(r, V)$ and projects probabilistic Monte Carlo trajectories via damped Euler-Maruyama SDEs ($\lambda = 0.99$).

01. 5-Layer Continuous-to-Physical Architecture

Rather than fitting discrete empirical curve parameters, Kinetopus lifts noisy market observables $(P_t, V_t)$ onto a smooth, $C^2$-differentiable continuous manifold and identifies governing differential equations through five sequential layers:

LAYER 0-1 Signal Lift
State Space & FFT Sensing
Converts prices to scaled log-returns $\tilde{r}_t = 100 \ln(P_t/P_{t-1})$ and volume Z-scores. Evaluates RFFT to detect dominant cyclical harmonics ($T_{\max}$).
LAYER 2 Continuity
Topological Splines + Liquid Memory
Fits cubic B-Splines $S(t) \in C^2(\mathbb{R})$ inversely weighted by local EWMA volatility. Derivatives $\dot{X}(t)$ and $\ddot{X}(t)$ are derived analytically.
LAYER 3 Isolation
Online CUSUM Regime Slicing
Monitors standardized residual drift $(S_t^+, S_t^-)$. Slices historical momentum at $H=4.0$, discovering equations exclusively over the active regime $\mathcal{R}_{\text{active}}$.
LAYER 4 Discovery
SINDy Sparse Equation Discovery
Solves STLSQ over polynomial library $\Theta(r,V)$ to extract explicit ODEs: $\dot{r} = c_0 + c_1 r + c_2 V + c_4 (r \cdot V)$.
LAYER 5 Stochastics
Damped Euler-Maruyama SDE ($M=1000$)
Integrates 1,000 Monte Carlo paths stabilized by hydrodynamic dissipation factor $\lambda=0.99$: $X_{t+1} = \lambda [X_t + f(X_t)\Delta t] + \Sigma_{\text{res}} \sqrt{\Delta t} \mathcal{N}(0, I)$.
$$X_{t+\Delta t}^{(m)} = \lambda \left[ X_t^{(m)} + f_{\text{SINDy}}\left(X_t^{(m)}\right) \Delta t \right] + \Sigma_{\text{res}} \sqrt{\Delta t} \, \mathcal{N}^{(m)}(0, I_2), \quad \lambda = 0.99$$
